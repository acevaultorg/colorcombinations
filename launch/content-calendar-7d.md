# 7-day post-launch content calendar

The launch day blast (HN + LinkedIn + Bluesky thread) is a single peak.
The 7 days after it are where the flywheel either keeps turning or
stalls. This calendar gives you one ready-to-post item per day so the
archive stays visible without needing daily from-scratch writing.

Post each item on Bluesky first, then mirror to Mastodon / Threads /
LinkedIn (30–60 min between platforms, same as launch day).

>

## Then what?

After day +7, drop to one post every 3–4 days unless you have something
specific to say. The goal is presence, not saturation. The HN launch
traffic will have tapered by day 4-5 and organic SEO should start
showing up in week 2–3.

**Stop drip-posting if:**
- Engagement drops below ~3% response rate on the prior 3 posts
- You start feeling like you're forcing it

**Do start drip-posting again if:**
- You ship a new palette, a new blog post, or a new collection
- A specific plate gets an unexpected spike of traffic (surface it
  publicly — "plate 174 is suddenly getting traffic; anyone know
  why?")

## What NOT to post in the 7 days

- The bundle. Let the /shop link sit quietly in the site nav; do not
  aggressively promote the bundle on social in launch week. Selling
  too hard tanks the "gift to the community" framing that makes the
  launch work in the first place.
- Generic design quotes. Every designer-adjacent account does this.
  Stay specific to the archive.
- Competitor comparisons. Don't mention Coolors / Khroma / Paletton
  by name in a critical tone — that turns the community against you
  faster than anything else on social media.
